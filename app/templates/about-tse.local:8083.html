searchinTSE

about

How to use the tool? you copy the title of the submitted paper (or a topic) and get the most competent reviewers from the TSE author community via "Reviewers".

What criteria are used to suggest reviewers? The suggested reviewers fulfill the following conditions: 1) having published at least once in TSE 2) being senior enough 3) still being research active.

What is the reviewer credit? The amount of peer-review provided by somebody minus the amount of peer-review consumed.

What are the differences w.r.t the Manuscript Central reviewer search functionality?
1) Manuscript Central recommends beyond the TSE author community, it recommends in the whole IEEE / MC database
2) Manuscript Central uses an inferior recommendation algorithm
3) Manuscript Central does not decorate the Reviewer results with the reviewer credit.

Who maintains this tool? This is tool is by Martin Monperrus, from KTH Royal Institute of Technology.
